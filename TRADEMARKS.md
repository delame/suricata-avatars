# Name and brand policy

The avatar definitions in this repository are **open**: you may copy, adapt and use them for any purpose, including commercially, under [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE). That license covers the content only. It **does not** give you any right to our names or logo (CC BY 4.0, section 2(b)(2)).

Our brand marks are: **Suricata**, **Surykaty**, **Suricata Sentinel**, **Sentinel Check**, **Sentinel Live**, the meerkat logo, and the looks and voices of our avatars once they exist.

## You may

- Credit us as the source, factually. Recommended wording:
  > Based on the Suricata Sentinel avatar definitions (https://github.com/delame/suricata-avatars), CC BY 4.0. Changed by [you]. Not reviewed or endorsed by Suricata.
- Say that your project is "compatible with" or "built on" the Suricata avatar format.
- Write about Suricata, review it or criticise it.

## You may not

- Use our marks as the name, or part of the name, of your product, fork, avatar, channel or organisation.
- Suggest that Suricata reviewed, endorsed, sponsored or runs your project, or that your verdicts or fact-checks come from Suricata.
- Present changed definitions as Suricata's own. Every adaptation must say that it was changed (CC BY 4.0, section 3(a)(1)(B)).
- Use our marks or avatars for political campaigning, advertising for a party or candidate, or anything that spreads disinformation.
- Use our logo or avatar looks and voices without written permission.

## If something goes wrong

If an adaptation misuses our name, we will ask for the credit to Suricata to be removed. CC BY 4.0 requires you to do that when we ask (section 3(a)(3)). Misleading use of the marks can also be pursued under trademark and unfair-competition law.

Questions or permission requests: open an issue in this repository.

*This policy is part of the project's ongoing legal review and may be updated.*
